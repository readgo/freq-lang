Why Walking and Using Your Phone Is So Dangerous

Have you ever looked down at your phone while walking and nearly walked into something — or someone?

As smartphones have become a part of our daily lives, walking while using a phone has become increasingly common.And although many of us may believe we can walk safely while checking messages, reading social media or watching videos, this can cause accidents and injuries.

In Japan, walking while using a smartphone is known as _aruki-sumaho_. Mobile phone and railway companies have held campaigns warning people about the dangers of _aruki-sumaho_  for years, but accidents still happen.

Kazuhiro Kozuka from Aichi University of Technology says these accidents happen because our brains can't fully focus on two things at the same time.Our brains usually focus on what's more difficult or interesting — like what's on our phone screens — which can mean our eyes may even see things without our brains really noticing.

Kozuka also says that we move our eyes differently while walking and using smartphones.Instead of scanning left, right and ahead, he told _The Japan Times_, we  shift our focus between our screens and the area directly ahead of us.

As a result, we're more likely to miss any possible dangers at our sides.

In fact, the Tokyo Fire Department reported that 171 people were taken to the hospital by ambulance between 2021 and 2025 after accidents involving smartphone use while walking or cycling. That included 43 cases in 2025 alone.

And in some cases, _aruki-sumaho_ accidents have caused people to die.In 2021, a woman in Tokyo was hit and killed by a train after she failed to notice it while using her smartphone at a railway crossing.

Kozuka believes accidents could be reduced through public awareness campaigns and smartphone features that discourage phone use while walking.
