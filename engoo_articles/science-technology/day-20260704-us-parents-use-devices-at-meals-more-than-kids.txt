US Parents Use Devices at Meals More than Kids

American parents use media devices during family meals even more than their children do, according to a new study published in the journal _JAMA Pediatrics_.

In 2025, researchers surveyed almost 360 parents in the US who have children aged between 4 and 10 years old.Seventy percent of parents said their children use devices during family meals — and 75% of parents said they themselves use devices during family meals as well!

The survey also found that individual smartphone use is much more widespread than the use of shared screens like TVs.And while a family might share an experience when they all watch something together on a TV, they lose that chance at connection when they use small screens.

The authors of the study say that family mealtimes are an important opportunity for parents to connect with their children, to check how they are doing and to socialize with them.Previous research also suggests that children who consistently share meals with their families have better eating habits and improved well-being.

But Cecilia Sada Garibay, one of the authors of the study, told _CNN_ that when everyone is on their own device, it means that each family member "is doing something absolutely different to the others."

"If you have your device and you're constantly checking it at the table, it can affect a valuable moment parents have with their children in the day, and it can have some effect on the relationship they have with their children," she said.

Some parents may not even realize how much time they spend looking at their devices.

Sada Garibay said she knows it's normal for parents to be very busy — but this also means it's more important than ever to make connections during family meals!
