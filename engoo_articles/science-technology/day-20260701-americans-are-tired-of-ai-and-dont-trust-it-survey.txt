Americans Are Tired of AI and Don't Trust It — Survey

How do you feel about AI?Do you find it useful?How much do you trust what it tells you?

A new survey has found that 86% of Americans don't fully trust information they get from AI, and 42% specifically said they don't trust AI when it doesn't show its sources.

Talker Research surveyed 1,200 American adults about their online experiences with AI.And not only do they not trust AI — they don't find it very helpful, at least when it's used by businesses.

Many businesses now use AI chatbots to answer users' questions about the company or its products.But 75% of participants said they find humans more helpful than AI when asking for information on a business website.

And if companies hope to pretend they're not using chatbots, it might not be so easy — 56% of participants said they can tell if they're chatting with a human or with AI.

Americans think chatbots are taking over the internet.The average American already believes that 55% of all the interactions they have online are with AI, and three out of four said the internet feels less human than it did 10 years ago.

They're especially seeing lots of AI on social media.Ninety-two percent of survey participants said they have come across AI or bots when using social media, and 63% said they come across them often.

And those bots aren't making users happy.Survey participants said that after about 40 minutes, they start to feel tired by all the bot-created content they see on social media.

So while businesses may be using more AI, they might want to do so more judiciously — because users are getting tired of it.People know when they're talking to AI, they don't find it very helpful, and they just don't trust it.
