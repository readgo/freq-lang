Kamakura Store Sells Kanji Ice Cream That Doesn't Melt
One of the difficult things about studying Japanese is the thousands of kanji characters you have to learn. But a new store in Kamakura may be able to help.

At the start of May, a store called Kanji Ice opened in the city. The store sells ice cream that's molded into kanji characters. You can buy an ice cream that says "Kamakura" in kanji, and others that say "Japan" and "ninja."

OK, this might not help you learn many kanji — but it's a fun way to practice reading even a few, right?

The store can also make other words in kanji characters for special occasions. The ice creams are available in three flavors, including chocolate, milk and strawberry.

But the kanji isn't the only thing that's interesting about the store. Because these characters are also made with ice cream that doesn't melt!

The store says the ice cream won't melt even after 30 minutes at room temperature. It adds that this is great because it lets people take the ice cream to places in Kamakura that are popular with tourists, and share photos of it on social media.

A Japanese company called Fulllife has been working with the store to create the ice cream. It uses polyphenols from strawberries to keep water and oil in the ice cream from separating. This helps stop it from melting.

Polyphenols are natural chemicals found in many fruits and vegetables.

The ice cream isn't only available in Kamakura. It's also available in a number of other places around Japan, including Sanga Stadium in Kyoto.
